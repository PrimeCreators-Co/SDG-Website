Health, Innovation, and Infrastructure

Focusing on two critical goals for a sustainable future: Good Health & Well-being and Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure.

Explore the Goals

SDG 3: Good Health & Well-being

Ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages.

Key Targets

  • Reduce maternal mortality.
  • End preventable deaths of newborns and children.
  • Fight communicable diseases.
  • Ensure universal access to sexual and reproductive health-care services.

Good health is essential to sustainable development. SDG 3 aims to achieve universal health coverage and provide access to safe and effective medicines and vaccines for all.

SDG 9: Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

Build resilient infrastructure, promote inclusive and sustainable industrialization and foster innovation.

Key Targets

  • Develop sustainable and resilient infrastructure.
  • Promote inclusive and sustainable industrialization.
  • Enhance scientific research and upgrade technological capabilities.
  • Increase access to information and communications technology.

Investment in infrastructure and innovation are crucial drivers of economic growth and development. SDG 9 aims to build a future where everyone has access to the technology and infrastructure they need to thrive.

Our Mission & Vision

Our commitment to a sustainable and equitable future.

Our Mission

To advocate for and contribute to the achievement of Sustainable Development Goals 3 and 9 by promoting universal health coverage, investing in resilient infrastructure, and fostering innovation. We aim to create a world where everyone has the opportunity to live a healthy life and benefit from sustainable industrialization.

Our Vision

We envision a future where good health and well-being are a reality for all, and where industry, innovation, and infrastructure drive sustainable economic growth and social progress. A world where no one is left behind, and where technology and collaboration solve the most pressing challenges of our time.

Why It Matters

The impact of our focus on global well-being.

Health is a Human Right

Access to quality healthcare is fundamental to human dignity and well-being. By focusing on SDG 3, we are working to ensure that everyone, regardless of their circumstances, can lead a healthy and productive life.

Infrastructure is the Backbone of Society

From transportation and communication to energy and sanitation, resilient infrastructure is essential for a functioning society. SDG 9 provides the framework for building a sustainable and inclusive future for all.

Innovation Drives Progress

New ideas and technologies are critical for solving the world's most pressing challenges. By fostering innovation, we can accelerate progress towards the SDGs and create a more sustainable and equitable world.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered.

What are the Sustainable Development Goals?

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a set of 17 global goals established by the United Nations in 2015. They are a universal call to action to end poverty, protect the planet, and ensure that all people enjoy peace and prosperity by 2030.

Why focus on SDG 3 and 9?

SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being) and SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure) are deeply interconnected. A healthy population is essential for a productive workforce, while a robust infrastructure is necessary to deliver healthcare services and drive economic growth. By focusing on these two goals, we can create a virtuous cycle of development.

How can I get involved?

There are many ways to get involved. You can start by educating yourself and others about the SDGs. You can also support organizations that are working to achieve the goals, or you can take action in your own community. Every little bit helps!
